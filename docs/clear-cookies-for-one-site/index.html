Clear cookies for one site in Chrome

Fix one site without signing out of every other site.

When a site won't let you log in, keeps showing an old page, or says "something went wrong", the usual advice is to clear its cookies. Clearing all of Chrome's cookies works too, but it signs you out of everything. You only need to clear the one site.

The quick way, with cookieZ

  1. Open the site in Chrome.
  2. Click the cookieZ icon. The first time, it asks for access to websites.
  3. Under the delete options, choose This page for the cookies this page gets, or This domain and its subdomains to include addresses such as login.example.com as well.
  4. Check the count. It says how many cookies will go and which sites they belong to.
  5. Click Delete, then Yes, delete. Then reload the page.

Deleted the wrong thing? Click Undo in the message at the top. It works for 10 minutes. Cookies you've marked as Kept are never deleted, so you can keep one login while you clear the rest.

Still can't log in?

Sites also store things outside cookies: local storage, databases and offline files. Click Clear and reload under the delete options. It deletes the site's cookies, clears everything else it has stored, and reloads it. Undo can put the cookies back, but not the rest.

Chrome's own way

Chrome can do this without an extension, a few menus deep. In recent versions:

  1. Click the icon at the left of the address bar.
  2. Click Cookies and site data, then Manage on-device site data.
  3. Click the bin next to each site you want to clear.

You can also type chrome://settings/content/all into the address bar, search for the site and click Delete data. Chrome doesn't show you the cookies themselves, and there's no undo.

Will it sign me out?

Yes, of that site. Logins are kept in cookies, so you'll need to sign in again. Other sites aren't affected.
