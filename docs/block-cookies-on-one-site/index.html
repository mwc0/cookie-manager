Block cookies on one site in Chrome

Choose what one site may store, and leave every other site alone.

Blocking every cookie on every site breaks most logins. Usually you want something narrower: one site that can't save anything, or a site that keeps your login but not the tracking cookies other companies put on its pages.

With cookieZ

  1. Open the site in Chrome and click the cookieZ icon.
  2. Click Block at the top. The first time, Chrome asks to let cookieZ change your cookie settings.
  3. Under On and the site's name, choose one:
    • Allow all cookies: what Chrome normally does.
    • Clear when Chrome closes: the site works as normal, but Chrome deletes its cookies every time it closes.
    • Block other companies' cookies: the site keeps its own cookies, so signing in still works. Embedded videos, maps and "sign in with" buttons from other companies may stop working.
    • Block all cookies: the site can't set or read any cookie. You'll be signed out, and it may not work at all.
  4. Blocking doesn't remove cookies the site already has, so cookieZ offers to delete them. Click Delete them or Leave them.

Chrome does the blocking, not cookieZ, so the rule keeps working while cookieZ is closed and applies in incognito windows too. Uninstalling cookieZ removes its rules.

Every site at once

The same screen has a section for every other site: block other companies' cookies everywhere, or block every cookie except on the sites you allow. There's also a switch to block about 130 known trackers' cookies on every site, from a list inside cookieZ that's never downloaded.

A site stopped working

Click Block on that site, choose Allow all cookies or remove its rule from the list, then reload the page.

Chrome's own way

Type chrome://settings/content/siteData into the address bar and add the site under the list of sites not allowed to save data. That blocks everything the site stores, not just cookies.
