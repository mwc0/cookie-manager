Support

Common questions, and how to report a problem.

Common questions

Why does it ask for access to all websites?

Chrome won't show an extension a site's cookies unless the extension has permission for that site. A cookie editor that works on any site needs permission for every site. cookieZ asks for it the first time you open it, not at install, and you can say no. You can also take it back later from the extension's Details page in chrome://extensions.

It says "No cookies on this page" on a page that has cookies

Chrome doesn't let extensions read cookies on its own pages, such as chrome:// pages and the Chrome Web Store. Switch to a normal website and open cookieZ again.

It doesn't open in incognito

Chrome turns extensions off in incognito unless you allow them. Go to chrome://extensions, open cookieZ's Details, and turn on "Allow in incognito". Incognito cookies are kept separate from your normal ones, and deleting in one never touches the other.

Is there a keyboard shortcut?

Yes. Press Alt+Shift+K to open cookieZ, / to jump to the search box, and Esc to go back from the editor, export, import or help. In the table, the arrow keys move between cookies, Enter edits one, Space ticks it, K keeps it and Delete deletes it, after asking. To change the first one, go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts. If another extension already uses Alt+Shift+K, Chrome leaves cookieZ without one, and you can pick your own there.

What does Keep do?

The bookmark button on each row marks a cookie as Kept. A cookie marked as Kept is left out of every delete cookieZ makes, including "All sites". It only affects cookieZ's own delete buttons. The website that set the cookie can still change or remove it.

I deleted or changed the wrong cookies. Can I get them back?

Yes, for 10 minutes. This works for a delete, an import, or a cookie you saved in the editor. Click Undo next to the message, or open cookieZ again and click Undo at the top. Only the most recent change can be undone. To make this possible, cookieZ holds the cookies as they were in memory for those 10 minutes. They're never written to disk, and closing the browser clears them.

I set an expiry date and it changed

Chrome won't keep a cookie for more than about 400 days, and quietly shortens anything longer. cookieZ tells you when this happens, so the date you see is the one Chrome actually stored.

Does "All sites" really delete everything?

Yes, apart from any cookies you've marked as Kept. It includes partitioned cookies, which many tools miss. Before deleting, cookieZ shows how many cookies will go and which sites they belong to. Cookies in incognito are separate and aren't touched from a normal window.

How do I move cookies to another browser or tool?

Choose which cookies in the delete panel, then click Export. JSON works with cookieZ and other cookie editors. cookies.txt works with curl, wget, yt-dlp and similar tools. The Playwright option makes a storageState file for starting automated tests already logged in. To bring cookies in, click Import and paste them, or open cookieZ in a tab to choose a file. Import also takes Set-Cookie lines, a Cookie header (which goes on the site you're on) or a curl command from "Copy as cURL" in DevTools. To copy cookies from one site to another, such as from staging to localhost, open cookieZ on the site they should go to, and tick "Put them on this site instead". You'll see how many cookies will be added, and for which sites, before anything changes. An export includes login cookies, so treat it like a password.

What does the size next to the cookie count mean?

It's roughly how much the site's cookies add to every request your browser sends it. Many servers refuse requests with more than about 8 KB of them, and the usual symptom is a site that won't let you log in, so cookieZ warns you above 6 KB. Deleting that site's cookies usually fixes it. A cookie marked Large is close to Chrome's 4 KB limit for a single cookie.

How do I remove it?

Right-click the cookieZ icon and choose "Remove from Chrome", or remove it from chrome://extensions. This also deletes the two things it saved: your Kept list and your theme choice. Your cookies stay as they are.

Report a problem

Please open an issue on GitHub, or if you'd rather not, email support@cookiez.uk. It helps to include:

  • your Chrome version (from chrome://version)
  • the version of cookieZ (shown in chrome://extensions)
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead

Don't paste cookie values into an issue. Issues are public, emails can be forwarded, and a session cookie can be used to log in as you. The cookie's name and the site are usually enough.
