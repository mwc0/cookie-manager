            Help
            
        
      
            

      
            
        
            Which delete option should I pick?
            
        
            
          This page deletes the cookies this page gets.
          This domain and its subdomains also reaches
          subdomains, such as login.example.com.
          All sites deletes every cookie in this browser
          profile. The count and the sites are always shown first.
        
            

        
            I deleted or changed the wrong cookies
            
        
            
          Click Undo in the message at the top. It works
          for 10 minutes after a delete, an import or a Save, even if you
          close cookieZ, and only for the most recent one.
        
            

        
            Why did a row light up?
            
        
            
          The site, or cookieZ, has just added or changed that cookie. The
          table follows changes as they happen, so you don't need to press
          Refresh.
        
            

        
            What does the bookmark do?
            
        
            
          It marks a cookie as Kept, so cookieZ leaves it out
          of every delete, even "All sites". It doesn't stop the website
          changing or removing the cookie itself. To see every cookie you've
          kept, on any site, click Kept cookies under the
          delete options.
        
            

        
            What do the badges mean?
            
        
            
          Secure: only sent over HTTPS.
          HttpOnly: hidden from the page's own JavaScript.
          Subdomains: also sent to every subdomain.
          Partitioned: kept separately for each site it's
          used on. SameSite: when other sites can send it.
          Large: close to Chrome's 4 KB limit for one cookie.
        
            

        
            What's the size next to the cookie count?
            
        
            
          Roughly how much this site's cookies add to every request. Many
          servers refuse more than about 8 KB, which can stop you logging in,
          so cookieZ warns you above 6 KB.
        
            

        
            Keyboard
            
        
            
          
            
            Alt + Shift + K
            Open cookieZ. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
            /
            Search the cookies.
            ↑ ↓
            Move between cookies. Home and End jump to the first and last.
            Enter
            Edit the cookie.
            Space
            Tick it.
            K
            Keep it, or stop keeping it.
            Delete
            Delete it. It asks "Sure?" first: press Enter to delete.
            Esc
            Go back from this screen, the editor, export or import, or cancel a delete.
          
            
        
            

        
            Incognito
            
        
            
          Turn on "Allow in incognito" on cookieZ's Details page in
          chrome://extensions. Incognito cookies are kept separate, and a
          delete in one never touches the other.
