            Help
            
        
      
            

      
            
        
            Which delete option should I pick?
            
        
            
          This page deletes the cookies this page gets.
          This domain and its subdomains also reaches
          subdomains, such as login.example.com.
          All sites deletes every cookie in this browser
          profile. The count and the sites are always shown first.
        
            

        
            What does a site store on me?
            
        
            
          Site report, beside the cookie count, groups a
          site's cookies by purpose and lists anything worth checking. Each
          purpose is a guess from the cookie's name.
        
            

        
            Clearing cookies didn't let me log in
            
        
            
          Try Clear and reload under the delete options, then
          Clear and reload. As
          well as the site's cookies, it clears what else the site has
          stored: local storage, databases, cache storage and offline files.
          Kept cookies are left alone, and Undo can put the cookies back, but
          not the rest.
        
            

        
            I deleted or changed the wrong cookies
            
        
            
          Click Undo in the message at the top. It works
          for 10 minutes after a delete, an import or a Save, even if you
          close cookieZ, and only for the most recent one.
        
            

        
            Why did a row light up?
            
        
            
          The site, or cookieZ, has just added or changed that cookie. The
          table follows changes as they happen, so you don't need to press
          Refresh. Click changes next to the cookie count
          to see a list of what was added, changed, removed or expired since
          you opened cookieZ.
        
            

        
            What does the bookmark do?
            
        
            
          It marks a cookie as Kept, so cookieZ leaves it out
          of every delete, even "All sites". It doesn't stop the website
          changing or removing the cookie itself. To see every cookie you've
          kept, on any site, click Kept cookies under the
          delete options.
        
            

        
            Can I stop a site setting cookies?
            
        
            
          Yes. Click Block at the top. For each site you
          can allow all cookies, have them cleared when Chrome closes, block
          other companies' cookies, or block every cookie. You can also block
          other companies' cookies on every site, or every cookie on every
          site except the ones you allow. Chrome does the blocking,
          so it works while cookieZ is closed, and removing cookieZ removes
          the rules. Chrome has its own switch for blocking other
          companies' cookies everywhere, in its settings; cookieZ doesn't
          change that.
        
            

        
            A site stopped working after I set a rule
            
        
            
          Click Block on that site and choose
          Allow all cookies, or remove its rule from the
          list. Then reload the page.
        
            

        
            What do the badges mean?
            
        
            
          Secure: only sent over HTTPS.
          HttpOnly: hidden from the page's own JavaScript.
          Subdomains: also sent to every subdomain.
          Partitioned: kept separately for each site it's
          used on. SameSite: when other sites can send it.
          Large: close to Chrome's 4 KB limit for one cookie.
        
            

        
            What's the size next to the cookie count?
            
        
            
          Roughly how much this site's cookies add to every request. Many
          servers refuse more than about 8 KB, which can stop you logging in,
          so cookieZ warns you above 6 KB.
        
            

        
            Keyboard
            
        
            
          
            
            Alt + Shift + K
            Open cookieZ. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
            /
            Search the cookies. Start with name:, value:, domain: or path: to search that part only.
            N
            Add a new cookie.
            ↑ ↓
            Move between cookies. Home and End jump to the first and last.
            Enter
            Edit the cookie.
            Space
            Tick it.
            K
            Keep it, or stop keeping it.
            Delete
            Delete it. It asks "Sure?" first: press Enter to delete.
            Esc
            Go back from this screen, the editor, export, import or site rules, or cancel a delete or other question.
          
            
        
            

        
            Can cookieZ stay open while I browse?
            
        
            
          Yes. Click More (the three dots at the top), then Open in side
          panel. cookieZ opens at the
          side of the window and shows the cookies of whichever tab you're
          on. It waits while you're editing, and catches up when you're
          back. The keyboard shortcuts work there too.
        
            

        
            Incognito
            
        
            
          Turn on "Allow in incognito" on cookieZ's Details page in
          chrome://extensions. Incognito cookies are kept separate, and a
          delete in one never touches the other.
